Dear Catherine, Forgive my long silence; the days have been full. I have begun a small kitchen garden of my own with peas and runner beans. With every good wish, Rose.
Dear Lillian, It has been some time since I last wrote. The weather has been unkind, with rain every afternoon for a fortnight. With every good wish, Helen.
Dear Charles, There is much to tell since my last letter. The schoolmaster's wife has had her child, a boy, and both are doing well. I remain your loving friend, Albert.
